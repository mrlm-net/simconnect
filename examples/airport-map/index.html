Quick start

  1. Type an ICAO and Load (the simulator must be running; ↻ fetches again).
  2. Traffic tab: pick Kind and Runway, click a stand on the map (or tick free stand); a departure can pick an Entry, an arrival an Exit. The route shows in magenta.
  3. ▶ Spawn. The aircraft gets a card under Aircraft; give it its clearances there.
  4. Or press ▶ Start game and work all traffic yourself.

Clearances

Pushbackpush back and start-up approved
Taxitaxi to the holding point / to the stand, via the taxiways shown
route pointselect a card, click a point of its route: taxi and hold there (progressive taxi)
Crosscross the runway it holds short of
Line upline up and wait
Take-offcleared for take-off (from the holding point: a rolling take-off)
Depart nowturnaround: depart the parked arrival without waiting

When something is in the way

Hold positionstops as soon as it comfortably can; Taxi releases it
Go aroundon final, before touchdown: climbs out, flies a circuit, lands again
Abort take-offbefore V1: stops, vacates, back to the holding point. Lined up: cancels the take-off clearance
✕removes the aircraft (asks first)

Aircraft keep their distance on the ground by themselves: they queue and give way where routes cross. A departure waits for the tug to drive clear, and a pushback waits while traffic passes behind the stand.

Flight options

Hold at every clearanceoff: they clear themselves after a short wait
Fly the proceduresSID after take-off; arrivals start at a STAR entry
Flight planin New flight: LKPR → LOWW for a departure (SID, airways, cruise), LOWW → LKPR for an arrival (appears at the STAR entry on that side)
Turnaroundan arrival departs again after the dwell
De-icingon the stand before the push, or at a pad (Charts → De-icing pads, or via point 1); automatic when the weather is icing (the game uses automatic)
Custom taxi routevia points (click taxiways) and taxiway names, in order; never where the aircraft does not fit
