Quick reference

  

    

    
Quick start

    
    
      
  1. Airport chip (top left): type an ICAO and Load (the simulator must be running; ↻ fetches again).
    2. 
      
  2. Traffic → New flight: kind, runway (Active follows the runway in use from the weather; pick one to override), click a stand on the map or tick Free stand; a departure can pick an Entry, an arrival an Exit. The route is previewed on the map. Spawn.
    3. 
      
  3. Click a strip or an aircraft to open its panel; its clearances are grouped by phase, the next one first.
    4. 
      
  4. Or Schedule: start the timetable (airlines fly it by themselves; the boards show it), or the ATC game (you work all traffic).
    5. 
    

    
Clearances

    

      
Pushback (N E S W)push back and start-up approved; a facing ends the push facing that way where it can

      
Start upstart engines on the stand

      
Taxito the holding point / to the stand via the route shown; Continue taxi after Hold position

      
Route pointwith an aircraft selected, click a point of its route: taxi and hold there (progressive taxi)

      
Crosscross the runway it holds short of

      
Line up · behind landingline up and wait · line up behind the landing traffic

      
Take-offcleared for take-off (from the holding point: a rolling take-off)

      
Cleared to landlanding clearance on final

      
Depart nowturnaround: depart the parked arrival without waiting

    

    
When something is in the way

    

      
Hold positionstops as soon as it comfortably can; Taxi releases it

      
Go aroundon final, before touchdown: climbs out, flies a circuit, lands again

      
Abort take-offbefore V1: stops, vacates, back to the holding point. Lined up: cancels the take-off clearance

      
Removeremoves the aircraft (asks first)

    

    
Aircraft keep their distance on the ground by themselves: they queue and give way where routes cross. A departure waits for the tug to drive clear, and a pushback waits while traffic passes behind the stand. While a clearance is on its way, that aircraft's buttons are locked.

    
Flight options

    

      
Hold at every clearanceoff: they clear themselves after a short wait

      
Pushback tuga tug pushes the departure; its model, yaw and position can be set (empty: defaults)

      
Fly the proceduresSID after take-off; arrivals start at a STAR entry

      
Flight planLKPR → LOWW for a departure (SID, airways, cruise), LOWW → LKPR for an arrival (appears at the STAR entry on that side)

      
Turnaroundan arrival departs again after the dwell

      
De-icingon the stand before the push, or at a pad (Airport → De-icing pads, or via point 1); automatic when the weather is icing (the game uses automatic)

      
Custom taxi routevia points (click taxiways) and taxiway names, in order; never where the aircraft does not fit

    

    
Scheduled traffic

    

      
BoardsSTD/STA, → estimate when late, ⟲ turnaround (the arrival's aircraft departs again), status and why

      
heldground stop: departures stay on the stand while the taxiways are full

      
landing flowan arrival appears later to keep the gap on final

      
waitingits STAR entry or stand is taken: it tries again

      
enroutean arrival on its way on its flight plan (MSFS AI), handed to the arrival at its STAR entry

      
Overflightstraffic between airports outside the area crossing it at cruise level

      
Other trafficMSFS AI and other add-ons (Map, off by default): the schedule respects it (default) or ignores it; × removes one

    

    
Map

    

      
Show my aircraftcentres the map on your aircraft

      
World viewthe traffic picture's circle, airports in range and every aircraft with call sign, level and phase (again: back to the airport)

      
Full screen · panelfull screen with the panel (Esc leaves) · hide the panel for the map alone

      
Show · Follow · Cameraon the selected aircraft: centre it · keep it in the middle (Esc stops) · the simulator camera follows it

      
clicka stand: pick it for New flight · our aircraft: select it · other traffic: details · a taxiway or point: raw data

      
Sequencethe landing sequence per runway with its controls, the tower and the conflicts; the final's spacing on the map, green kept, red short

Keys

1–6switch section
